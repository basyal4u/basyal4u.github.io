Ganga Prasad Basyal, PhDAssistant Professor of Information Systems
Professional biography

Research that connects models to meaningful decisions.

Ganga Prasad Basyal, PhD is an Assistant Professor of Information Systems in the College of Business & Information Systems at Dakota State University. He earned his PhD in Information Systems from Dakota State University in 2025, specializing in Analytics & Decision Support.

His scholarship centers on artificial intelligence, machine learning, deep learning, data analytics, and information systems, with particular emphasis on healthcare and medical imaging. His doctoral work examined how medical-image characteristics, data quality, and data quantity influence transfer-learning effectiveness and computational performance.

His broader research portfolio spans natural language processing, healthcare knowledge management, text analytics, healthcare quality indicators, generative AI in higher education, and data-driven decision support. He combines technical model evaluation with an information-systems perspective: how data, algorithms, people, and institutions jointly shape outcomes.

Alongside research, he is committed to student mentorship and applied learning. His teaching philosophy emphasizes conceptual clarity, experimentation, responsible AI reasoning, and the confidence to build.

“Research should create useful knowledge; teaching should make that knowledge usable.”
