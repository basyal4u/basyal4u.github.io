Future course modules · ready to use

Advanced AI Lab Studio

Eight lightweight modules prepared for future classes. These are concept-first browser simulations: useful for instruction, discussion, and assignment design without pretending to be production AI services.

AI literacy · moved from BADM-201

AI or Human? Calibration Challenge

Students make authorship judgments, record confidence and evidence cues, then compare accuracy with calibration. The goal is to understand why writing style alone is not reliable proof of AI authorship.

Future-course direction: expand the sample bank, add evidence-quality comparisons, and connect the exercise to responsible academic-integrity review.
Generative AI · moved from BADM-201

LLM Sampling Explorer

Students manipulate temperature, top-k, top-p, and token logits to see how sampling controls reshape a next-token probability distribution.

Future-course direction: connect sampling behavior to real model outputs, evaluation, reproducibility, and agentic workflow design.
Computer vision

CNN Image Classifier

Toggle simple image patterns and observe an edge-based toy classifier.

Bias & Fairness Lab
