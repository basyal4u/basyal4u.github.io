AI literacy · moved from BADM-201

AI or Human? Calibration Challenge

Students make authorship judgments, record confidence and evidence cues, then compare accuracy with calibration. The goal is to understand why writing style alone is not reliable proof of AI authorship.

Future-course direction: expand the sample bank, add evidence-quality comparisons, and connect the exercise to responsible academic-integrity review.
Supervised learning · extended module

k-NN Classification

Explore neighborhood size, distance metrics, query location, feature scaling, and decision boundaries in an instance-based model.

Use it for: comparing local versus global behavior after completing the six BADM-201 graded labs.
Optimization · extended module

Gradient Descent

Compare learning rate, momentum, starting position, and convex versus non-convex loss surfaces.

Use it for: connecting training dynamics to the neural-network lab without duplicating its graded assignment.
Unsupervised learning · extended module

K-Means Clustering

Investigate centroid initialization, k, inertia, iteration, and failure on non-spherical data.

Use it for: extending the course into segmentation and unsupervised learning.
