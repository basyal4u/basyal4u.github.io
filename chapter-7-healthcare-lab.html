5 · D2L SUBMISSION

Generate your evidence report

50-point rubric

  • 10 pts — explains the confusion matrix and clinical error costs
  • 12 pts — records at least six controlled runs
  • 10 pts — compares threshold and prevalence effects
  • 10 pts — evaluates subgroup performance and human review
  • 8 pts — provides a defensible recommendation and limitation

Required evidence: case code, six runs, exact settings and outputs, two written comparisons, knowledge-check score with corrections, and deployment recommendation.

Student evidence rule: Submit your own explanation of the runs you performed. Do not use generative AI to write or rewrite the analysis unless your instructor explicitly permits it.
