Doctoral research

Data characteristics × transfer-learning effectiveness.

The dissertation investigates how the characteristics of medical-image data affect the effectiveness of multi-stage transfer learning for MRI classification.

The work combines a deep-learning artifact, timeline-based literature synthesis, comparative CNN evaluation, and a focus on training efficiency when medical datasets are small or heterogeneous.
