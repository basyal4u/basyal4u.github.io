Student innovation showcase

How to add a project.

Student work appears only after faculty approval. The process is designed to keep quality, privacy, and academic integrity standards consistent.

Step 1

Build something worth showing

Complete a course or research project with a clear problem, method, evidence, and reflection.

Step 2

Prepare the showcase package

Submit a 100–150 word project summary, one image/screenshot, semester/course, team member names you are authorized to publish, and optional demo/GitHub link.

Step 3

Faculty review

Dr. Basyal reviews academic quality, attribution, privacy, licensing, and whether the work is appropriate for public display.

Step 4

Revise if needed

Make requested changes and confirm that all team members approve the public listing.

Step 5

Publish to the showcase

An approved project receives a consistent public card with project title, context, description, contributors, and verified links.
